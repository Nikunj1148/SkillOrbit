# 🎮 OXOX Arena (Tic-Tac-Toe)

The OXOX game has been built and is ready to play in `game.html`!

### 🌟 Features Included:
- **Futuristic Cosmic Neon Design**: Glowing cyan (X) and magenta (O) with sleek dark glassmorphism.
- **Game Modes**:
  - 🤖 **vs AI**: With 3 selectable intelligence levels (Easy, Smart, and Unbeatable Minimax).
  - 👥 **2 Players (PvP)**: Local pass-and-play.
- **Audio Synthesizer**: Procedural sound effects using Web Audio API (Zero external assets needed).
- **Celebration Confetti**: Dynamic particle burst on winning rounds.
- **Scoreboard & Hotkeys**:
  - `Space` or `R`: New Round
  - `M`: Mute / Unmute Sound

### 🚀 How to Play:
Double-click `game.html` to open it in your web browser, or launch it with your local browser.
